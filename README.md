# 🎓 Master’s Thesis: Gender Bias and Sentiment in YouTube Discourse on Femicide in Italy

This repository contains the full research project and materials for my master’s thesis, which investigates how gender bias and sentiment are expressed in Italian YouTube comments related to femicide (femminicidio) and gender-based violence (violenza di genere). The study combines computational methods with critical interpretation to explore how digital discourse reflects or challenges dominant narratives.

## 🔍 Research Questions

- How is gender bias expressed in public discourse about femicide and GBV on YouTube?
- What is the relationship between gendered language and sentiment?
- How do public sentiments correlate with real-world femicide events?
- What recurring themes emerge in online discourse, and how might they influence public understanding or policy?

## 🧠 What This Research Is About

Using over 25,000 YouTube comments posted between 2019 and 2025, this thesis applies transformer-based sentiment analysis (FEEL-IT), topic modeling (LDA), and awareness detection to examine how Italian digital audiences engage with gender-based violence. The study is grounded in a technofeminist framework and emphasizes the ethical and interpretive challenges of analyzing emotionally charged, ideologically contested discourse.

## 📊 Key Findings

| Insight                          | Impact                                                                 |
|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| ✅ Negative sentiment dominates  | Reflects emotional intensity and institutional distrust               |
| 💬 Feminist terms are contested  | Used both supportively and sarcastically                              |
| 📈 Sentiment spikes mirror events| Peaks align with high-profile femicide cases                          |
| 🧠 Shared vocabulary             | Topic structures consistent across sentiment categories               |
| ⚠️ Awareness ≠ agreement        | Keyword presence does not guarantee ideological alignment             |

## 🧪 Methods

- **Data**: 25K+ YouTube comments (2019–2025)
- **Sentiment Analysis**: FEEL-IT (transformer-based, Italian)
- **Topic Modeling**: Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA)
- **Awareness Detection**: Custom keyword-based rule system
- **Temporal Analysis**: Aligned with 2024 femicide dataset from Non Una Di Meno

## ⚠️ Limitations

- No demographic metadata due to YouTube API restrictions
- Sarcasm and irony remain difficult to detect computationally
- Keyword-based awareness detection cannot infer stance
- Platform-specific bias (e.g., male-dominated user base)

## 🚀 Future Directions

- Cross-platform comparison (e.g., TikTok, X/Twitter)
- Emotion detection and hate speech filtering
- Multilingual replication studies
